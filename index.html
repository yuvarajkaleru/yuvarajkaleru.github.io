AI-Powered Interview Assistant

In progress · final-year project

An interview platform that reads a candidate's resume, matches it against a job description, then runs a voice interview that tests each skill the resume claims, and reports which claims held up.

  • LLM-generated technical and behavioural questions per claimed skill, with follow-ups based on the answers
